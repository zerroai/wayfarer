How it works

From a price to a payment in four steps.

01

The shop prices the sale

The merchant enters an amount. Wayfarer locks the rate in NEAR for 60 seconds and shows a payment request.

02

You see exactly what you pay

Your phone shows the price in your own currency and in NEAR, plus the vault it comes from and your limit.

03

Approve with a touch

One fingerprint signs a post-quantum cheque. It crosses to the shop by NFC tap, with QR codes as a backup.

04

The shop confirms, then settles

The shop verifies the cheque offline, hands over the goods, and the vault contract settles it on NEAR.

Features

Built to be safe, simple and checkable.

Post-quantum

Signed to last

Every payment is an ML-DSA-65 cheque, the NIST post-quantum standard, verified on-chain by the NEAR vault contract.

cheque 5.4 KB · 6 QR frames
verify ml_dsa_verify on-chain
Limits

A stolen phone can’t empty your vault

The phone’s key can only spend inside the mandate you set: a cap per payment, a daily cap, an end date. It can’t withdraw or change policy.

Order agent

Say it. The menu prices it.

The shopkeeper says “one coffee”. An AI on NEAR AI Cloud picks items from the shop’s own menu. The total is computed in code, never by the model, and it can’t move money.

Receipts

Check any sale in a browser

Each sale leaves a receipt that links the request, signature, mandate and on-chain events. 20 checks run right in the page.

At the counter

No internet needed to accept

The shop checks the signature offline and shows “accepted, pending settlement”. Final payment happens when it settles on NEAR.

Cash-out

Paid in local currency

One button turns NEAR into USDC and pays toward a bank account. Shown here with a sandbox rail; no licensed partner is integrated yet.
